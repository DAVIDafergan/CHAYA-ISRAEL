# **App Name**: Chaya Impact

## Core Features:

- Secure Donation Processing: Enable quick and recurring donations through leading US payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal) supporting USD transactions. Includes selection of fixed donation amounts.
- Donor Dashboard & History: Provide a personalized donor dashboard for viewing donation history, downloading aggregated tax receipts for tax season, and managing recurring contributions.
- Project Showcase Pages: Dedicated landing pages for each philanthropic project (e.g., Warm Blankets, Food Boxes), featuring detailed information and a direct donation button for that specific cause.
- Dynamic Impact Reporting: Visually display real-time impact numbers, such as food packages distributed or orphans supported, and feature a blog section with compelling success stories and field updates.
- AI-Powered Impact Story Tool: A generative AI tool that assists in crafting impactful and empathetic narratives for blog posts and impact stories, maintaining a respectful and empowering tone that avoids pity and focuses on partnership, based on provided project data.
- CRM & Email Marketing Integration: Automate donor data synchronization with CRM systems like Salesforce or HubSpot and connect with Mailchimp for targeted email newsletters and updates.
- Mobile-First & Accessibility Compliance: Ensure the platform is fully responsive for optimal viewing and interaction on mobile devices, and complies with international WCAG accessibility standards.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: A deep, dignified blue (#1E4099) to convey trust, stability, and gravitas, aligning with the foundation's respected mission.
- Background Color: A clean, nearly-white off-white (#F9FAFC) with a subtle blue tint, providing ample contrast for readability and a fresh, spacious feel.
- Accent Color: A clear, serene cyan-blue (#2BD1F0), strategically used for calls-to-action and highlights to evoke clarity, impact, and a touch of modern grace.
- Headline Font: 'Alegreya' (humanist serif) for an elegant, intellectual, and contemporary feel. Body Font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for warmth, modern readability, and professional clarity.
- Utilize clean, modern, line-based icons for clarity and professionalism. Incorporate subtle gold or soft green hues within select iconography or decorative elements to reflect the user's desire for 'touches of gold/green' and emphasize hope and positive change.
- A mobile-first, grid-based responsive layout with generous white space ensures a clean, respectful, and organized presentation. Information is presented in clear, digestible blocks or cards to enhance user experience across all devices.
- Subtle, smooth transition animations for page navigation and hover effects on interactive elements like buttons and cards to create a sophisticated and engaging user experience without distractions.
